About Nyore

Nyore is a mobile app for Android and iPhone. Point your camera at an artwork, object or building and Nyore tells and narrates its story; you can ask follow-up questions, plan a day or a multi-day trip around museums and places, explore with friends in Group Mode, play quizzes and share recaps of your visits. Some features are free; others need Nyore Premium.

AI-generated content

Most of what Nyore tells you (identifications, stories, answers, narration, day plans, trips, quizzes and group summaries) is generated by artificial intelligence. AI can be wrong: it may misidentify a work, get a date, attribution or fact wrong, describe something that does not exist, or suggest a place that has moved or closed.

  • Treat it as a knowledgeable guide, not an authority. Check what matters to you with the venue or another reliable source.
  • Opening hours, prices, ticket rules, routes and walking or travel times are estimates. Check them before you go, and use your own judgement about safety on the way.
  • Nothing in Nyore is professional advice: not an art appraisal, authentication or valuation, and not legal, medical, financial or safety advice.
  • Follow the rules of the places you visit, including rules about photography.

Group Mode

In Group Mode, what you add (your display name and picture, messages, questions, photos, pins, check-ins, votes and scores) is shared with the members of that group. Be kind and respectful, share only what you are happy for the group to see, and do not post photos of other people who have not agreed to it. If you delete your account, your entries stay in groups under the name "Former member" and your photos are removed.
