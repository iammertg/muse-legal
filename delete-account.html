Delete your account

Last updated November 21, 2026. For Nyore: Your AI Cultural Companion, by Mert Can Gözükara.

You can delete your Nyore account and the data that comes with it at any time, on Android or iPhone, in the app or by email. Deletion is permanent: a deleted account cannot be restored.

In the app

  1. Open Nyore and make sure you are signed in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Tap the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm with Delete. Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

By email, without the app

If you no longer have the app, or cannot sign in, send an email to gozukara.mert1@gmail.com with the subject Account Deletion Request, from the email address of the Google or Apple account you signed in with. If you used Apple's "Hide My Email", say so and we will find your account with you.

We reply to confirm your request and delete the account within 7 business days, and we email you when it is done. We never ask for your password.

What is deleted

  • Your account and profile: name, email, sign-in identifiers, interests and quiz answers
  • All your chats, with the photos you took or chose and their narration audio
  • Your day plans, multi-day trips, saved places and visit history
  • The photos you shared in groups: pins, question pictures, bingo squares and album items
  • Your sign-in tokens, notification records and registered devices

What is kept, and for how long

Your check-ins, questions, votes and game scores in a group
Stay for the other members, under the name "Former member", so their group and its rankings still make sense. Your photos are removed from them.
A day plan a group or another member is still using
Stays with them, without its public share link.
App events recorded on our servers
Up to 90 days, then only weekly totals with no link to you.
AI usage records (no message text)
Up to 30 days. Daily usage counters: up to 60 days.
Feedback messages you sent
Kept while we need them to handle your report. Ask in your deletion email and we delete them too.
Product analytics (PostHog) and crash reports (Sentry)
Expire under those services' retention periods. Ask in your deletion email and we delete what is linked to your account.

Details are in our privacy policy.

Deleting some data only

You do not have to delete your whole account to remove some of your data. In the app you can delete a single chat, with its photos and narration, from the Chats tab, and remove saved places and plans. For anything else, such as your feedback messages or your analytics data, email us at gozukara.mert1@gmail.com and say what you would like deleted.

Your subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel Nyore Premium. Cancel it in the store you bought it from: on Android, in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions; on iPhone, in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
