Privacy Policy

📅 Effective: February 26, 2025 📱 App: Muse (com.frontendmuse.app)

Overview

Muse ("we", "our", or "us") is an AI-powered assistant designed for frontend engineers. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the Muse mobile application ("App").

By using Muse, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the App.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Account information – email address and profile details via Auth0 authentication.
  • Usage data – interactions with the App, session activity, and feature usage.
  • Camera & photo library – images you voluntarily share for AI analysis (processed and not stored permanently).
  • Microphone & voice input – voice queries for speech recognition features (processed in real-time, not stored).
  • Device information – device model, OS version, and app version for diagnostic purposes.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide AI-powered engineering assistance and responses.
  • To authenticate and manage your account securely.
  • To improve the App's features and performance.
  • To process voice and image input for in-App functionality.
  • To respond to support requests.

Third-Party Services

Muse uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Auth0 – authentication and identity management.
  • OpenAI / AI providers – processing your queries to generate responses.
  • Expo / EAS – app delivery and over-the-air updates.

We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

Data Retention

We retain account information for as long as your account is active. Voice and image inputs are processed in real-time and are not permanently stored. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

Children's Privacy

Muse is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child has provided us personal data, we will delete it promptly.

Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including encrypted data transmission and secure authentication. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Your Rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw consent for data processing where applicable.
  • Lodge a complaint with a relevant data protection authority.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated effective date.
