Privacy policy

Last updated November 21, 2026. Applies to Nyore on Android and iPhone.

This policy explains what Nyore: Your AI Cultural Companion ("Nyore", "we", "us") collects when you use the app on Android or iPhone, why, who processes it on our behalf, how long it is kept, and how you can delete it. We do not sell your data, we show no ads, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Who we are

Nyore is made by Mert Can Gözükara, an individual developer based in Leipzig, Germany, who is the controller of your personal data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). You can reach us at gozukara.mert1@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google (on Android and iPhone) or with Apple (on iPhone), we receive your name, email address and a unique account identifier from that provider. We never see your Google or Apple password.
  • Photos. When you take or choose a photo to identify an artwork, object or place, it is sent to our servers and to Google Gemini for analysis. We also store the photo in our private cloud storage (Cloudflare R2) with the chat it belongs to, so it stays in your chat history. It is deleted when you delete that chat or your account. Photos you share in Group Mode (pins, pictures sent with group questions, bingo squares and album items) are stored the same way and can be seen by the members of that group.
  • Your questions and chat history. What you type or say to the assistant, its answers and the narration audio we generate for them are stored on our servers so your chats are there when you come back. You can delete any chat in the app.
  • Voice input. When you speak to Nyore, your phone's built-in speech recognition (Google on Android, Apple on iPhone) turns your voice into text. Depending on your device, this happens on the phone or on Google's or Apple's servers, under their privacy policies. We receive only the resulting text, never a recording of your voice.
  • Location. If you allow it, the app reads your device's precise location (accurate to about 100 metres) while you are using it, and sends those coordinates to our servers to find museums and places near you and to put the stops of a route in a sensible order. We use it to answer that request and do not save it to your account. Location is optional: you can deny it and pick a city instead.
  • Plans, places and visits. The day plans and multi-day trips you create, the places you save, favourite or mark as visited, your visit history, quiz scores and recaps.
  • Your interests. Your answers to the taste quiz when you set up the app, the interest tags you choose, and a short summary of your interests written by the AI from them, used to personalise suggestions.
  • Group Mode. When you join a group, the other members see your display name and picture, your check-ins, pins, questions, votes, messages, game scores and the photos you share in it.
  • Usage and analytics. The app records how you use it: screens viewed, key actions (such as generating a plan, saving a place or sharing a recap), session length, and the campaign link you arrived from, if any. These events are linked to your account identifier and go to two places: PostHog, our product analytics service, and our own servers, which keep a smaller set of events. They are never used for advertising or sold. The analytics switch in Settings → Privacy stops the PostHog events; the events our own servers record are not affected by it and are kept for 90 days (see How long we keep it).
  • Diagnostics. Crash reports and performance traces (from 10% of sessions) go to Sentry so we can fix bugs. They are linked to your account identifier; your email address is obscured on your phone before a report is sent.
  • AI usage records. For each AI request we log which feature and model were used and how much text was processed (not the text itself), linked to your account, to control cost and enforce fair-use limits.
  • Subscription status. Whether you have Premium, when it renews or ends, and whether you are in a trial. Payments are handled by Google Play or Apple; we never receive your card or bank details.
  • Feedback. If you send feedback or report an answer from the app, we receive your message, the email address you give (if any), your app version, device model, operating system, language, the screen you were on and your last few app events, so we can reproduce the problem.
  • Notifications (iPhone). If you allow notifications, we store your device's push token and notification preferences, with its time zone, language and app version, so a notification arrives at a sensible local hour and in your language. Trip and in-visit reminders are scheduled by your phone and never leave it. Nyore does not send push notifications on Android yet.

How we use it

  • To identify artworks and places, answer your questions and narrate the answers
  • To find places near you, build day plans and trips, and order their stops
  • To sign you in, keep your chats, plans and groups across devices, and run Group Mode
  • To personalise suggestions, quizzes and recaps
  • To unlock Premium when you have a subscription
  • To send the notifications you allow, and only those: never marketing or promotional pushes
  • To find and fix bugs, keep the app fast, prevent abuse and keep AI costs under control
  • To understand which features are used, so we can improve them
  • To answer your support requests

Under the GDPR we rely on these legal bases: providing the app you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b)), for your account, chats, photos, plans, groups and subscription; your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), for location, camera, microphone and notifications, which you can withdraw at any time in your phone's settings; and our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) in keeping the app secure, stable and affordable and in understanding how it is used, for diagnostics, AI usage records and analytics. You can object to the latter at any time (see Your rights).

Services that process it

We use the following providers to run Nyore. Each receives only what it needs for its part, and each is governed by its own privacy policy.

  • Google Gemini (Google): the AI that analyses your photos and generates answers to your questions, day plans and trips, quizzes and group summaries. It also creates some of the narration voices. Receives your photos, your questions and the context needed to answer them. We use the Gemini API on Google's paid tier: under its terms, Google processes this content as our processor to provide the service and does not use it to improve its products. (Gemini API Terms, Google Privacy Policy)
  • Cartesia: generates narration voices from the text of the assistant's answers. (Cartesia Privacy Policy)
  • Google Places and Google Maps: details, photos and directions for venues, and the maps shown in the app. Our servers send them place searches, which can include coordinates near you. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim: place and city searches. Our servers send it your search and an area of a few kilometres around your location; it never receives your account details. (OSMF Privacy Policy)
  • Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple: signing in. (Google, Apple)
  • Google Play Billing and Apple App Store: buying and managing your Premium subscription. (Google, Apple)
  • RevenueCat: keeps track of your subscription status across the stores, using your Nyore account identifier. (RevenueCat Privacy Policy)
  • PostHog (EU data region): product analytics, as described above. (PostHog Privacy Policy)
  • Sentry (EU data region): crash reports and performance monitoring. (Sentry Privacy Policy)
  • DigitalOcean and MongoDB Atlas: the servers and database that run Nyore. Cloudflare R2: private storage for photos and narration audio. (DigitalOcean, MongoDB, Cloudflare)
  • Apple Push Notification service: delivers notifications on iPhone, sent directly from our servers. (Apple Privacy Policy)

Pictures of artworks and places in the app are loaded from Google and Wikimedia Commons, which, like any website, see your device's IP address when they serve an image.

Some of these providers process data outside the European Union, including in the United States. Where they do, the transfer relies on the safeguards the GDPR provides for, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

We do not sell your personal data, do not share it with anyone for advertising, and do not engage in cross-app or cross-website tracking. Nyore does not collect advertising identifiers (no Android advertising ID, no IDFA).

How long we keep it

Account, interests, chats, plans, trips, saved places, visit history
Until you delete them or your account.
Photos in a chat, and their narration audio
Until you delete that chat or your account.
Photos you shared in a group
Until you delete your account.
App events recorded on our servers
90 days, then only weekly totals with no link to you.
AI usage records
30 days. Daily usage counters for fair-use limits: 60 days.
Notification records
30 days after they are created.
A registered iPhone (push token and settings)
180 days after it last opened the app, and at once when you sign out on it or turn notifications off in iOS Settings.

PostHog analytics and Sentry diagnostics
For those services' retention periods. They are not removed automatically when you delete your account; ask us and we delete them.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: open the Profile tab, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account. This deletes your account and profile, your chats with their photos and narration, your plans, trips, saved places and visit history, your group photos, your sign-in tokens, notification records and registered devices.

A few things stay, so that other people's groups keep working: your check-ins, questions and game scores in a group remain for its members under the name "Former member", and a day plan that a group or another member is still using stays with them. App events, AI usage records and feedback are kept for the periods listed above.

No app? See how to delete your account by email. Deleting your account does not cancel a Premium subscription: cancel it in Google Play or the App Store.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, and similar laws where you live, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy of it
  • Have it corrected or deleted
  • Restrict its processing, or object to processing based on our legitimate interests
  • Withdraw a consent you gave, at any time, without affecting what was done before
  • Receive the data you gave us in a portable format
  • Complain to a data protection authority, such as the Saxon Data Protection and Transparency Commissioner (Sächsische Datenschutz- und Transparenzbeauftragte) or the authority where you live

To exercise any of these rights, email us from the address you signed in with. We answer within one month.

Children's privacy

Nyore is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we learn that a child has given us personal data, we delete it promptly.

Security

All data travels over encrypted HTTPS connections. Sign-in tokens are kept in your phone's secure storage, and photos are stored privately and shown only through short-lived links. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and fix weaknesses as soon as we find them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Nyore changes. We will update the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app before they take effect.
