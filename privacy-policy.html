Privacy Policy

Effective immediately for all users

This Privacy Policy describes how Nyore — Your AI Cultural Companion ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application ("App"). By using Nyore, you agree to the practices described below.

01Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name, email address, and a unique user identifier to create and manage your account.
  • Images: Photos you capture or select from your library are sent to our servers solely for AI-powered cultural and artwork analysis. Images are processed in real time and are not stored permanently on our servers.
  • Voice & Audio Data: If you use the voice mode, audio is processed for speech recognition during your session only and is not retained afterward.
  • Location Data: With your permission, we access your approximate location (when the app is in use) to show nearby museums, venues, and help you plan your day. Location data is not stored on our servers.
  • Chat History: Your conversations with the AI assistant are stored on our servers to provide continuity across sessions. You may request deletion at any time.
  • Activity, Itinerary & Group Data: Your visit history, planned itineraries, group rooms, mini-game scores, and shared favorites are stored to personalise your experience.
  • Usage Data: We collect anonymised data about how you interact with the app (features used, session duration) to improve our service.
  • Subscription Status: We store whether you hold an active premium subscription to unlock premium features.

02How We Use Your Information

  • To provide AI-powered artwork and cultural analysis
  • To authenticate and manage your account securely
  • To show nearby museums and venues based on your location
  • To personalise itineraries, recommendations, and quiz content
  • To persist your chat history and group activity across sessions
  • To manage your subscription and unlock premium features
  • To improve the App's features and performance
  • To respond to support requests

03Third-Party Services

Nyore uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Apple Sign-In — authentication via your Apple ID (Apple Privacy Policy)
  • Google Sign-In — authentication via your Google account (Google Privacy Policy)
  • RevenueCat — in-app purchase and subscription management (RevenueCat Privacy Policy)
  • Google Maps — displaying museum locations and nearby points of interest (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Expo / EAS — app delivery and over-the-air updates (Expo Privacy Policy)

We do not sell your personal data to any third party, and we do not share your data with third parties for advertising purposes.

04Data Retention

Images and voice data are processed in real time and are not permanently stored on our servers. Account information, chat history, group activity, and itinerary data are retained as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

05Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing where applicable
  • Lodge a complaint with a relevant data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

06Children's Privacy

Nyore is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child has provided us personal data, we will delete it promptly.

07Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including HTTPS encryption for all data in transit, secure token storage on your device, and JWT-based authentication with automatic token refresh.

08Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
