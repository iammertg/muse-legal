Frequently asked questions

What is Nyore?

Nyore is your AI cultural companion. Point your camera at anything (a painting, a sculpture, a temple, a hidden corner of any city) and Nyore reads its story out loud. You can ask follow-up questions, plan a full day around one anchor stop, tour with friends, and share a recap of every visit.

How do I create an account?

Download Nyore from Google Play or the App Store and open it. On Android, tap "Sign in with Google"; on iPhone, choose "Sign in with Apple" or "Sign in with Google". There is no Nyore password: signing in runs through Google or Apple directly.

How does the AI assistant work?

Nyore uses large language models to answer your questions in real time. You can type your question, speak it with voice mode, or share a photo for visual analysis (brushwork, era, provenance, historical context). Your questions and photos are analysed by Google Gemini, and your chats, with their photos, are kept so you can come back to them, until you delete them. Voice is turned into text by your phone's speech recognition; we never receive a recording.

Why does Nyore need camera and microphone access?

Camera and photo permissions let you point at any artwork or object, or choose a photo, for AI-powered visual analysis. The microphone lets you ask questions hands-free, useful when you're walking through a gallery and don't want to type; your phone's own speech recognition turns your voice into text. Both are optional, and you can change them at any time in your phone's settings.

How does Plan Your Day work?

Anchor one stop (a museum, a café, a landmark you've been saving) and Nyore composes the rest of the day from curated venues, balanced pace, and walking-time math. You can adjust the duration, the number of stops, and the vibe (relaxed, balanced, packed) and re-generate as many times as you like.

What is Group Mode?

Group Mode lets you and your friends tour the same place together. You'll get live chat, shared favorites, mini-games (Bingo and Fact-or-Bluff), a leaderboard, and a single shared recap at the end of the day. One person creates the room, the rest join with a code.

Is my data private and secure?

We never sell your data, never share it for advertising and show no ads. Photos you take are stored privately with your chat until you delete it, we never receive recordings of your voice, and location is used only for nearby places and routes. Our privacy policy lists everything we collect, who processes it and how long it is kept.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open the Profile tab, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account, then confirm. Without the app, email gozukara.mert1@gmail.com from your sign-in address with the subject "Account Deletion Request"; we delete it within 7 business days. What is deleted and what is kept.

How do I cancel Nyore Premium?

Premium is managed by the store you bought it from. On Android, open Google Play, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Premium stays active until the end of the period you've paid for. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel it. See our terms for renewals and trials.

Which languages does Nyore support?

Nyore speaks 18 languages, including English, Turkish, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Arabic, Persian, Hindi, Bengali, Punjabi, Telugu, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Vietnamese, and Javanese. The interface and voice narration both adapt automatically.

How do I share my visit recap?

After any visit, open the Recap from your activity feed. Choose Story, Carousel, or Post. Nyore generates a ready-to-share image with the venue, your favorite pieces, the numbers that mattered, and a QR code back to the experience. Save it or share straight to your feed of choice.

The app isn't working as expected. What should I do?

First, force-quit and reopen the app, then check your internet connection. If the issue persists, try uninstalling and reinstalling. If none of that helps, please email gozukara.mert1@gmail.com with a short description, your phone model, and its Android or iOS version. We'll get back to you quickly.
