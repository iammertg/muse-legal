Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?
Download Muse from the App Store and open the app. Tap "Sign Up" and follow the prompts to create an account using your email address via our secure Auth0 authentication.
How does AI assistance work in Muse?
Muse uses large language models to answer your frontend engineering questions in real-time. You can type your question, use voice input, or share an image for visual analysis. All queries are processed securely and are not permanently stored.
Why does Muse need camera and microphone access?
The camera and photo library permissions allow you to share screenshots or images for AI-powered visual analysis. The microphone permission enables voice input so you can ask questions hands-free using speech recognition.
Is my data private and secure?
Yes. We take your privacy seriously. Voice and image inputs are processed in real-time and are not permanently stored. Your account data is secured via Auth0. Please review our Privacy Policy for full details.
How do I delete my account?
To delete your account and all associated data, please email us at support@frontendmuse.app with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". We will process your request within 7 business days.
The app isn't working as expected. What should I do?
First, try closing and reopening the app, then check your internet connection. If the problem persists, try uninstalling and reinstalling the app. If none of these steps work, please contact us at support@frontendmuse.app with a description of the issue and your device model and iOS version.
